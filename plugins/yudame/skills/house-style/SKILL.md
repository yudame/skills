---
name: house-style
description: "Fallback visual direction when the project has no style guide. Use before choosing fonts, colors, or layout for anything visual."
---

The project's own style guide, design system, or brand always wins; look for one first (the repo, the artifact design systems, the client's brand). Only when there is none, use this:

Black ink on warm cream, Lora serif headings over a quiet sans body with small mono labels, structure from hairlines and whitespace instead of boxes, square corners, no shadows or gradients, and one red used like an architect's red pen on the single thing that matters.
